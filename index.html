About Us

River Town Crooks was founded by two non-traditional university students who shared a relentless passion for heavy metal and wanted to express their unique life experiences through raw, thunderous soundscapes. What started as late-night jam sessions in a damp basement has rapidly evolved into a fierce four-piece powerhouse.

Having added two like-minded musicians to round out their heavy, groove-laden rhythm section, the band continues to refine their aggressive yet melodic style. Driven by gritty riffage and honest storytelling, River Town Crooks looks forward to making their mark on the local scene and connecting with fans across the region.
